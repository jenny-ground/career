# 기초 회화 패턴 은행 (Pattern Bank)

## 왜 패턴부터 하나
비즈니스 문장은 **기초 패턴 + 업무 단어**로 되어 있다.

- Today **we'll go over** the new pickup schedule. = **We'll ~** (~할 거예요) + **go over** (살펴보다) + 업무 단어
- **What time works for you?** = **What time ~?** (몇 시에) + **~ works for you** (~가 괜찮다)

틀(We'll ~)이 입에 붙어 있지 않으면, 업무 문장 하나하나가 새로 외울 문장이 된다. 그래서 틀을 쉬운 일상 문장으로 먼저 입에 붙이고, 그다음 업무 단어만 바꿔 끼운다.

## 수업에서 쓰는 법
- 패턴 수업 1회 = 패턴 1개(익숙해지면 2개). 선생님 지침 4-3번 방식.
- **바꿔 말하기 드릴**: 한국어 문장을 듣고 그 패턴으로 바로 영어로 말하기. 일상 6개 → 업무 4개 순서. 아래 예문 외에 선생님이 새 문장을 만든다.
- **숙달 기준**: 같은 문장을 외워서 맞히는 게 아니라, 매번 **처음 보는 새 문장**을 그 패턴으로 바로 말할 수 있으면 맞힌 것. 다른 날 3번 연속이면 숙달.
- 핵심 표현 표에는 패턴을 `[패턴] We'll ~.`처럼 적고 종류를 '패턴'으로 한다.

## 묶음 1 · 부탁하고 제안하기

| 번호 | 패턴 | 뜻 | 일상 예문 | 업무 예문 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | Could you ~? | ~해 주시겠어요? | Could you open the window? / Could you help me? | Could you send me the file? / Could you say that again? |
| P02 | Let me ~. | 제가 ~할게요 | Let me see. / Let me help you. | Let me check. / Let me share my screen. |
| P03 | Let's ~. | (같이) ~해요 | Let's eat lunch. / Let's go home. | Let's meet online. / Let's start the meeting. |
| P04 | I'd like to ~. | ~하고 싶어요 (정중) | I'd like to order coffee. / I'd like to rest. | I'd like to schedule a meeting. / I'd like to ask a question. |
| P05 | I'll ~. / We'll ~. | ~할게요 / ~할 거예요 | I'll call you tonight. / We'll eat out on Friday. | I'll send it today. / Today we'll go over the schedule. |

## 묶음 2 · 질문하기

| 번호 | 패턴 | 뜻 | 일상 예문 | 업무 예문 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P06 | Are you ~? | ~예요? (상태) | Are you hungry? / Are you busy now? | Are you available next Tuesday? / Are you ready? |
| P07 | Do you ~? / Do you have ~? | ~해요? / ~ 있어요? | Do you like coffee? / Do you have time? | Do you use our system? / Do you have the data? |
| P08 | What time ~? / When ~? | 몇 시에 / 언제 | What time do you get up? / When is your birthday? | What time works for you? / When is the pickup? |
| P09 | Is it ~? / Is that ~? | ~예요? | Is it cold outside? / Is that your bag? | Is that okay with you? / Is it on Friday? |
| P10 | How about ~? | ~는 어때요? | How about pizza? / How about this weekend? | How about 9 a.m.? / How about Wednesday? |

## 묶음 3 · 대답하고 말하기

| 번호 | 패턴 | 뜻 | 일상 예문 | 업무 예문 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P11 | ~ works for me. / ~ doesn't work for me. | ~가 괜찮아요 / 안 돼요 | Saturday works for me. / 7 p.m. doesn't work for me. | Tuesday at 10 works for me. / That doesn't work for me. |
| P12 | I think ~. | ~ 같아요 | I think it's going to rain. / I think he's tired. | I think that works. / I think we need more time. |
| P13 | I can ~. / I can't ~. | ~할 수 있어요 / 없어요 | I can swim. / I can't come today. | We can get the data. / I can't hear you. |
| P14 | I have to ~. / I need to ~. | ~해야 해요 | I have to go now. / I need to sleep. | I need to check the schedule. / We have to finish by Friday. |
| P15 | I'm ~ing. | ~하는 중이에요 | I'm cooking. / I'm on my way. | I'm checking the data. / We're working on it. |

## 묶음 4 · 지난 일과 상태

| 번호 | 패턴 | 뜻 | 일상 예문 | 업무 예문 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P16 | I ~ed. / Did you ~? | ~했어요 / ~했어요? | I watched a movie. / Did you eat? | I sent the file. / Did you get my email? |
| P17 | I haven't ~ yet. / Have you ~ yet? | 아직 안 했어요 / 벌써 했어요? | I haven't eaten yet. / Have you finished? | We haven't received the POD yet. / Have you checked it yet? |
| P18 | There is ~. / There are ~. | ~가 있어요 | There is a cafe near here. / There are two kids. | There is a problem. / There are three shipments. |
| P19 | It's ~. | (시간·날씨·상태) ~예요 | It's late. / It's sunny today. | It's on Friday. / It's two hours late. |
| P20 | I was ~. / It was ~. | ~였어요 | I was tired. / It was fun. | The truck was late. / It was a good meeting. |

## 묶음 5 · 업무 동사 덩어리

| 번호 | 패턴 | 뜻 | 일상 예문 | 업무 예문 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P21 | go over ~ | ~을 살펴보다 | Let's go over the menu. / I'll go over my notes. | Today we'll go over the schedule. / Let's go over the data. |
| P22 | get back to ~ | ~에게 다시 연락하다 | I'll get back to you tonight. | Let me check and get back to you. |
| P23 | send ~ to ~ / send me ~ | 보내다 | Send me the photo. / I'll send it to my mom. | Could you send me the file? / We send updates every 30 minutes. |
| P24 | set up ~ | (회의를) 잡다, 설정하다 | Let's set up a dinner. | Can we set up a meeting? / I'll set up the call. |
| P25 | check ~ / check on ~ | 확인하다 | I'll check the weather. | Let me check the ETA. / Could you check on the shipment? |

## 묶음 6 · 확인하고 마무리하기

| 번호 | 패턴 | 뜻 | 일상 예문 | 업무 예문 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P26 | Do you mean ~? | ~라는 뜻이에요? | Do you mean today? | Do you mean the pickup time? |
| P27 | Just to confirm, ~. | 확인차 말하면 ~ | Just to confirm, we meet at 7? | Just to confirm, the ETA is Friday? |
| P28 | I'm not sure ~. | ~인지 잘 모르겠어요 | I'm not sure he's home. | I'm not sure we can do that. |
| P29 | Thank you for ~. / Sorry for ~. | ~해서 고마워요 / 미안해요 | Thank you for dinner. / Sorry for being late. | Thank you for your time. / Sorry for the late reply. |
| P30 | Can I ~? | ~해도 될까요? | Can I sit here? / Can I try this? | Can I ask a question? / Can I share my screen? |

---

묶음 6까지 끝나면 원장이 학생 상태를 보고 다음 패턴 묶음(조건 if, 비교, 이유 because, 제안 should 등)을 추가한다.
